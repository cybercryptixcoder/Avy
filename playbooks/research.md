# Research
Finding out what's true: search widely, read the sources themselves, and say how sure you are.

1. Before searching, say to yourself what would answer the question, and what would change your mind.
2. Search in short queries, several angles (the thing's name, the problem it solves, who made it, the year). Batch
   independent searches in one go.
3. Snippets are leads, not facts. web_fetch the sources you'll rely on, and prefer primary ones: the official docs, the
   paper, the repository, the announcement, the dataset. One good source read fully beats five skimmed.
4. Check the claims that matter against a second independent source, especially numbers, dates and "the first/best/only".
5. Notice when sources disagree or are out of date; say so instead of picking one silently.
6. Answer the question first, in your own words, then where it came from (the addresses). Mark what you couldn't confirm.
7. Anything current (a version, a price, who holds a role, news) is searched, never answered from what you remember.
